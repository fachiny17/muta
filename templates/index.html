00 / Privacy and consent

A quick note before you start

Please understand what Muta does and what it saves.

  • Muta is an AI study assistant, not a human teacher. Answers can contain mistakes, so check important information with your teacher or course material.
  • We store an anonymous ID, your chosen language, question and answer text, ratings and timings. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your voice recording is deleted right after processing.
