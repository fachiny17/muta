Before you start

Muta is an AI study assistant, not a human teacher. Check important answers with your teacher.

We store an anonymous ID, your language, the text of your question and the answer, your ratings, and timing data. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email, and your recording is deleted right after processing.

Tap the microphone, speak, then tap again to stop.

Need an idea? Try asking one of these
  • What is the difference between stress and strain?
  • State the first law of thermodynamics.
  • Explain photosynthesis in simple words.
  • What is a noun? Give two examples.
