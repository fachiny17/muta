How to read these numbers

  • ASR / LLM success mean the speech-recognition and answer-generation steps ran without a technical failure. They do not measure whether an answer is correct; that is what the user ratings show.
  • Task completion is the share of students who said the answer helped them finish their task.
  • Participants are anonymous IDs; no names, phone numbers or emails are collected, and recordings are deleted right after processing.
  • Sample sizes are small, so percentages should be read with caution.
