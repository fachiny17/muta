How to read these numbers

  • ASR / LLM success means the speech recognition and answer generation stages completed without a technical failure. It does not measure correctness; user ratings provide a separate signal.
  • Task completion is the share of students who said the answer helped them finish their task.
  • Participants are anonymous IDs. Names, phone numbers and email addresses are not collected, and recordings are deleted right after processing.
  • Sample sizes may be small, so percentages should be interpreted with caution.
